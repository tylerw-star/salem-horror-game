THE REVEAL

The witch you saw was never a historical explanation for Salem.

The real horror was a community in which fear, authority, reputation, religious belief, rumor, and the pressure to conform could turn accusations into accepted truth.

In 1692, accusations of witchcraft spread through Salem and surrounding communities. Courts accepted forms of testimony that were later challenged, and dozens of people were accused, imprisoned, or executed before the prosecutions collapsed.

The final question is not simply, “Who was the witch?”

It is: How did Salem decide what counted as truth?
